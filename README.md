# Empires Rising

*A Minecraft Bedrock Edition addon about exploration, conquest, army-building, and faction warfare.*

Empires Rising fills the Overworld with enemy camps to discover, raid, and conquer.

Fight hostile mobs, collect corrupted resources, purify them during dangerous night-time events, and use the rewards to raise your own army.

Command Barbarians, Archers, and Dragons, join one of three rival factions, build a personal portal network, and take your troops wherever your empire expands.

**Want the full showcase?** [Watch the addon demo on YouTube](https://youtu.be/CDfpcTod6do).

3. Wait for Minecraft to import both the Behavior Pack and Resource Pack.

4. Create a new world or edit an existing world.

5. Activate eighter the **Empires Rising** resource or behaviour pack in the world settings.

6. Enter the world and begin exploring.

## Your Journey at a Glance

1. **Explore** the Overworld and discover fortified enemy camps.
2. **Raid** camps, defeat their defenders, and collect the loot inside.
3. **Hunt** hostile mobs for Corrupted Souls and Rotten Hearts.
4. **Purify** those resources under the open night sky.
5. **Defend** the Purifier until its work is complete.
6. **Craft** useful spawners with pure resources.
7. **Choose** the Fire, Water, or Void faction.
8. **Raise** an army of Barbarians, Archers, and Dragons.
9. **Upgrade** your troop spawners to field stronger forces.
10. **Command** nearby troops with the Troop Horn.
11. **Travel** through a personal network of named portals.
12. **Fight** monsters and rival factions as your empire grows.

## Collect Corrupted Resources

Defeating hostile mobs can reward you with **Corrupted Souls** and **Rotten Hearts**.

These materials cannot reach their full potential until they have been purified.

Keep hunting, gather as many as you can, and prepare for the night.

<img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 25" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/2773adb8-949f-4c61-9110-fa975b51e714" /> <img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 25 (1)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/89c4cbbb-fa6a-430c-b591-c63d10539504" />

## Purify Them at Night

Craft and place a **Purifier** beneath the open sky.

At night, interact with it and choose how many Corrupted Souls and Rotten Hearts you want to process.

Once activated, the Purifier begins turning them into **Pure Souls** and **Pure Hearts**.

<img width="489" height="220" alt="Screenshot 2026-08-27 2054082" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/aa81ef81-6609-470a-8d7c-886a9d8b75d6" /> <img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 25 (2)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5340dc9f-c0a1-464c-952d-42db0f480406" />

### Defend the Purifier

Purification is not peaceful.

Undead creatures are drawn to an active Purifier, and Empty Souls will approach it from the darkness.

Protect the Purifier until the process finishes to receive full rewards.

<img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 25 (3)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e25c3e5f-b4f7-4cbc-8cf3-62e653cbde3b" /> <img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 25 (5)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e2810f88-2ef8-46a0-bab3-5657dee5584f" />

If an Empty Soul reaches the Purifier, it will destroy it and interrupt the process.

Breaking the Purifier early also ends the event, and some unfinished resources may be lost.

<img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 25 (4)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6194d4ea-1fe0-4ae4-b35b-f8d4fdce6c56" />

### Hunt Empty Souls

Empty Souls can be killed before they reach the Purifier.

They are fragile, but they move faster as they get closer to their target.

Defeating one also gives you a rare chance to obtain a **Void Shard**, which unlocks the portal recipe.

## Raise Your Army

Pure Souls and Pure Hearts are used to create and operate troop spawners.

Craft a spawner, interact with it, and begin producing loyal units for your army.

If a troop dies, its spawner can produce a replacement as long as you queue another unit and have the required resources.

Breaking a spawner removes its linked troops and returns part of their cost.

The spawner item keeps its upgrades when picked up, stored, moved, and placed again.

## Choose Your Faction

Before raising your first troops, you must pledge yourself to one of three factions:

* **Fire** 
* **Water** 
* **Void** 

Your troops inherit your faction when they are created.

Players and troops in the same faction are allies, even when they belong to different players.

Troops from rival factions are enemies and will fight when they meet.

<img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 25 (8)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/58ccd0ef-ba7d-4b19-87e9-6ed8b67664d8" />

### Changing Factions

A **Faction Token** allows you to choose a different faction, get it from defeating bosses.

You must first destroy all troop spawners you own and disband your current army.

Only then can you leave your old faction and pledge yourself to another.

<img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 25 (9)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e7679ef0-c015-43af-ba0d-d446ce7100ac" />

## Upgrade Your Troops

Interact with a placed troop spawner to manage and improve the units it creates.

Spawner upgrades can improve troop equipment and increase the number of active units the spawner can support.

Upgrades progress through four material tiers: copper, iron, diamond and netherite.

<img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 25 (10)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/373a986f-61e4-4397-9e7e-c636cd383900" />

### Dragons

**Dragons** are powerful flying units with their own level upgrades.

They are expensive additions to an army, but their mobility and strength can change the course of a battle.

Get a Magma Core from defeating the Lava Golem to craft a Dragon Spawner.

### Place and Name a Portal

Place a portal with two blocks of vertical space available.

Give it a unique name so you can recognize it later in the destination menu.

Each player manages only their own portals.

<img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 38" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9dcee9cc-9bff-4f47-bd8c-928d10cc9078" />

### Choose a Destination

Interact with a portal to see your other available portals.

Select a destination to activate the entrance for a short time, then step inside to travel.

Portals can connect distant Overworld locations and can also take you between the **Overworld**, **Nether**, and **End**.

<img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 38 (1)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/268479fb-ceb6-4d5a-9498-3569e03d1cee" />

### Bring Your Army

Your following nearby troops travel with you through the portal.

This lets you move an army between bases, battlefields and camps without abandoning your forces.

They wont teleport between dimensions with you, only vanilla portals allow that.

<img width="426" height="240" alt="Video Project 38 (2)" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/abcebe99-4260-4541-9497-c101a8c9f6e9" />

### Bring the Portal Too

When choosing a destination, enable **Bring this portal with me** if you want to remove the entrance after traveling.

The portal is returned as an item, allowing you to place it again somewhere else.

## Ways to Play

### Become a Camp Raider

Explore constantly, track down the largest camps, defeat their bosses, and collect valuable loot.

### Build a Kingdom

Create defended settlements, station troops with the Troop Horn, and connect your territory with named portals.

### Lead a Faction Army

Join forces with players from your faction and fight rival armies in large multiplayer battles.

### Master Potions

Load the Potion Gun with the throwable potion that fits the situation, then support your troops or pressure enemies from range.

### Conquer Every Dimension

Use portals to move troops between the Overworld, Nether, and End, expanding your empire far beyond its starting point.

## Quick Tips

* Explore new Overworld territory to find more enemy camps.
* Search camps carefully; rewards may be spread throughout the fortifications.
* Start purification early enough in the night to finish the event.
* Keep watch in every direction while defending an active Purifier.
* Protect Archers behind Barbarians during large fights.
* Use the Troop Horn before traveling so nearby troops follow you.
* Long-press the Troop Horn when you want troops to guard an area or fight freely.
* Remember that the Potion Gun holds only one potion type at a time.
* Aim Milk Potions carefully because every splashed entity can lose its effects.
* Name portals clearly so their destinations are easy to identify.
* Keep troops near a portal before activating it if you want them to travel with you.
* Destroy all owned troop spawners before trying to change factions.
